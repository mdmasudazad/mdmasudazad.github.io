About Me

I earned my Bachelor of Science in Civil Engineering from Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET). My academic and research interests span seismic resilience, steel and composite structures, finite element analysis, structural health monitoring, multi-hazard assessment and Performance-Based Earthquake Engineering.

I am particularly interested in transforming structural simulation data into interpretable machine-learning tools that can support engineering analysis, prediction and decision-making.

Predictive Modelling of Horizontal Stiffness in Scrap-Tire Isolators

A hybrid finite-element and machine-learning framework developed to predict the horizontal stiffness of scrap-tire isolators using geometric, material and loading parameters.

  • Finite-element-generated structural dataset
  • Multiple supervised regression algorithms
  • XGBoost-based stiffness prediction
  • Model comparison and error evaluation
  • Feature-importance and sensitivity analysis
  • Interactive web-based engineering calculator
